# Asymmetry — Overview (verified facts)

> Source: public pre-beta disclosure at asymmetria.io. Only add content here that is
> verified and safe to share with testers. The Beta Support agent treats this folder
> as ground truth.

## What Asymmetry is
Asymmetry is a privacy-first system that tests **zero-knowledge packet delivery**
architectures. It is currently in **pre-beta**.

## Pre-release status
Asymmetry is pre-release, unaudited software. Testers should treat everything as a
test, keep their own backups, and expect features to change, break, or disappear
without notice. It should not be used on critical production data.

## Privacy and zero-knowledge
Data remains private and is **inaccessible to the company** — the company cannot see,
read, retrieve, or recover tester data. The zero-knowledge core is safety-critical.
However, the delivery pipeline itself is subject to unexpected downtime.

## Backups and data recovery
Always keep **external, local backups** of all encrypted payloads. The company
**cannot recover data lost** due to protocol crashes during the beta phase. Message
delivery is not guaranteed during beta; packets may be dropped or delayed.

## What Asymmetry is NOT
Asymmetry is not a wallet, account, exchange, custodian, bank, broker, or financial
adviser. Do not rely on this preview for safety-critical communication or financial
decisions.

## Liability
The software is provided "as-is." To the maximum extent permitted by law, the company
is not liable for any direct, indirect, or consequential damages, including lost
financial capital, dropped data packets, undelivered messages, or corrupted payloads
occurring during the beta testing period.
